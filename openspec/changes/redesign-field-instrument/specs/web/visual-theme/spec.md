## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: A look of its own
The site SHALL look like a field instrument: a light, sun-legible palette by default; legends set in small mono capitals; readouts in the mono face with tabular figures; white plates with a hairline border and corner registration ticks for cards and lists; a hi-vis accent reserved for action, focus, and the answer; a survey teal for data and linework. Every color SHALL come from a token, both modes SHALL meet WCAG AA for text and focus, and radii SHALL stay at or below 8 px.

#### Scenario: Terrain is decoration
- **WHEN** a reader has asked for reduced motion, or uses assistive technology
- **THEN** the home terrain draws one still frame and is hidden from the accessibility tree
