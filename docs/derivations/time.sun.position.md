# Sun position (NREL SPA) (`time.sun.position`)

## Method

The NREL Solar Position Algorithm (Reda and Andreas 2008): heliocentric Earth position from the VSOP87 periodic terms, nutation and aberration, apparent sidereal time, topocentric parallax, and refraction. ΔT from the Espenak and Meeus polynomials unless given; DUT1 optional.

## Equations

- Julian ephemeris day JDE = JD + ΔT/86400; heliocentric L, B, R from the periodic-term tables.
- Geocentric λ = L + 180° + Δψ + Δτ; apparent right ascension and declination from ε.
- Topocentric α′, δ′ by parallax; elevation e = e₀ + Δe (Bennett refraction with pressure and temperature).

## Symbols and units

JD Julian day (UT), ΔT = TT − UT (s), Δψ nutation in longitude, ε obliquity, α right ascension, δ declination, e elevation, Φ azimuth.

## Domain

Years -2000 to 6000 per the SPA. Refraction is applied only above -0.8333° apparent.

## Approximations

Stated SPA uncertainty ±0.0003°. Refraction uses standard atmosphere unless pressure and temperature are given.
